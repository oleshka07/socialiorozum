Privacy Policy - Holos by Rozum

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Service: Holos by Rozum (holos.rozum.one; formerly socialio, socialio.rozum.one) · Operator: Swipe Scape s.r.o. · Contact: privacy@swipescape.eu

Holos by Rozum ("Holos", "we") is a content tool for small businesses, experts and creators. It turns your own material - notes, voice messages, transcripts, articles, photos and videos - into social-media posts in your brand's voice and helps you schedule and publish them to the accounts you connect: Telegram, Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, Threads, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok and Google Business Profile. This policy explains what data we process, why, and how you can delete it.

Who we are. Holos is operated by Swipe Scape s.r.o., Chebská 38/5, Dvory, 360 06 Karlovy Vary, Czech Republic, company ID (IČO) 22269134, registered in the Commercial Register kept by the Regional Court in Plzeň, file C 47634. Swipe Scape s.r.o. is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Privacy requests: privacy@swipescape.eu.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: your email address and authentication data (password hash or Google sign-in identifier), and your login sessions.
  • Content you create or import: brand profile and strategy, materials (notes, transcripts, RSS and news articles, voice messages you send to our Telegram bot and their transcripts), posts you generate or edit, first comments, the publishing schedule, and photos and videos you upload.
  • Connected accounts: access tokens and basic account information (name, username, Page or account ID) for the platforms you choose to connect. Tokens are stored server-side and used only to act on your instructions.
  • Post statistics: for posts published through Holos, the metrics the platforms return (views, reach, likes, comments, replies, shares, saves) and your follower counts, so we can show you analytics.
  • Short links and bio page: if you turn on short links or publish a bio page (holos.rozum.one/@name), we count how many times each link was opened and how many times the page was viewed, per day. We do not store the IP address, device, cookies or any other data about the people who open them, and link-preview bots are not counted.
  • Technical data: service logs and AI usage records, used for security, troubleshooting and spending limits.
  • Cookies: one essential session cookie keeps you signed in (on our test environment, one more remembers its access PIN). We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use information

Only to provide the service: generate, edit and format posts; publish and schedule them to the accounts you connect, including the first comment you write; show analytics about your own posts; keep the product secure and support you. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Threads)

With your permission we access the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts you select, and your Threads profile, in order to: publish the posts, carousels, videos, reels and stories you approve; post the first comment you write under your own post; read statistics of your own posts; and show replies to your own Threads posts so you can answer them. We do not access personal Facebook timelines, friends lists or private messages. You can revoke access at any time in Facebook settings (Business Integrations) or in Threads (Settings → Account → Website permissions).

3a. TikTok

If you connect TikTok, Holos uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to read your basic profile (display name, avatar and account ID) and to send the videos you approve to your TikTok account - either as a draft in your TikTok inbox that you finish in the TikTok app, or as a direct post with the privacy, comment, Duet and Stitch settings and the commercial-content disclosure you choose before posting. We do not read your other videos, followers, comments or messages. We store the tokens and the IDs of the posts we sent. You can revoke access in your TikTok account settings (apps and services permissions) or by disconnecting TikTok in Settings → Channels, which deletes the stored tokens. Your use of TikTok remains subject to the TikTok Terms of Service.

3b. Telegram

Holos publishes to the Telegram channels and groups where you made our bot (or your own bot) an administrator. If you connect a bot to Telegram Business with the "Manage stories" right, Holos uses it only to post the stories you approve to your Telegram profile. We store the Business connection ID, your Telegram user ID, name and username, and which rights you granted. Our bot does not receive or read your Business chats.

Your own bot: if Telegram Business is already used by your own bot and you connect it to Holos "without takeover", Holos stores its token only to post your stories and to check the Business connection with Telegram; we never change its webhook or read its updates. Your bot itself forwards to Holos the Business connection and, only if you turn on "Ideas from my messages", the text of the messages you wrote. Messages written by other people are rejected even if a bot forwards them, unless you separately turn on "And client questions (anonymized)". With that option, Holos removes e-mail addresses, phone numbers, links, usernames and account, card or order numbers from each client message before storing it, and uses these messages only to find the topics clients ask about: post ideas never quote a client or describe a specific person. The forwarded text is kept without the name or ID of the person in the chat, is processed once a day to suggest up to three post ideas to your Idea bank, and is then deleted - in any case within 48 hours. Turning an option off or disconnecting the bot in Settings → Channels → Telegram deletes any text still waiting.

If you turn on client questions, you are the controller of your clients' messages and are responsible for having a legal basis and informing them (for example in your own privacy notice); Holos processes that text on your behalf and only for this purpose.

4. Google user data

Google Drive: Holos requests only the drive.file scope. Through the Google Picker you choose specific files or folders, and Holos imports images from them into your media library so you can attach them to posts.

  • We access only the files and folders you pick; we read those image files and basic file metadata (id, name, type) to import and de-duplicate them.
  • We do not access, read, or store other files in your Drive.
  • Imported images are stored in your Holos workspace; we keep the Google file ID to avoid importing duplicates.

YouTube (YouTube API Services): Holos uses YouTube API Services. If you connect YouTube, Holos requests the youtube.upload scope only to upload the videos you choose to publish as Shorts, with the title, description and the "made for kids" and "altered or synthetic content" settings you choose, and youtube.readonly only to show which channel is connected.

  • We store the channel name, the access tokens, and the IDs and links of the videos Holos uploaded for you. We do not read your other videos, comments, subscribers or watch history.
  • By using the YouTube features of Holos you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can revoke Holos's access at any time on the Google security settings page or by disconnecting YouTube in Settings → Channels, which deletes the stored tokens and channel data. The IDs of videos already published stay with your posts so you can open them, and are erased when you delete those posts or your account.

Google Business Profile: if you connect your Business Profile, Holos requests the business.manage scope and uses it only to:

  • list the business locations you manage, so that you choose which of them Holos may post to;
  • publish to the locations you selected the posts that you approved in Holos, and show you whether each post went out and its link.
  • We do not change your business information, opening hours or photos, and we do not read or reply to reviews.
  • We do not access locations you did not select.
  • We store only the IDs and names of the selected locations and the tokens needed to publish; disconnecting the profile in Settings → Channels deletes them.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer it, except as needed to operate the service at your direction.
  • No humans read your Google data except where you explicitly request support, where required for security, or to comply with law.
Limited Use disclosure. Holos's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. AI and infrastructure providers

To generate text, images, voice-overs and transcriptions, we send the relevant content (your material, brand settings and instructions) to the AI providers we use, such as OpenAI, Google (Gemini), OpenRouter, fal.ai, Cloudflare Workers AI, Deepgram and Microsoft Azure; stock photos come from Pexels. They process this data on our behalf to deliver the service. If you connect your own Claude (Anthropic) to Holos through our connector, your Claude reads and writes your workspace data at your direction.

6. Storage, security & retention

Data is stored on our private server (PostgreSQL and file storage) with nightly backups kept for 14 days. Tokens are kept server-side and are never shown to other users. We keep your data while your account is active. After a long period of inactivity we email you and may delete uploaded media; your brand settings stay.

7. Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Profile → Danger zone → Delete account. Your data is hidden immediately and permanently erased after a 14-day grace period (signing in again within 14 days cancels the deletion); backups roll off within the following 14 days. To remove only one connected account, use Settings → Channels → Disconnect, which deletes its stored token. You can also email privacy@swipescape.eu from your account email. Details: Data deletion instructions.

8. Sharing

We share content only with the platforms you explicitly publish to (Telegram, Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Google Business Profile) and with the service providers listed above, strictly to deliver the service. We do not share Google user data with third parties for their own purposes.

9. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected on this page. Questions and requests about your data: privacy@swipescape.eu (Swipe Scape s.r.o., Chebská 38/5, Dvory, 360 06 Karlovy Vary, Czech Republic). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů). See also our Terms of Service.
