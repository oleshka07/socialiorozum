Data Deletion Instructions

socialio (socialio.rozum.one) - an AI content tool that helps users create and publish posts to their own social accounts (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Telegram).

How to delete your data (in‑app)

  1. Sign in at socialio.rozum.one.
  2. Go to Налаштування → Небезпечна зона (Settings → Danger zone).
  3. Click «Видалити акаунт» (Delete account) and confirm with your email.

Your data is hidden from the cabinet immediately and permanently erased after a 14‑day grace period (logging back in within 14 days cancels deletion). Permanent deletion removes your workspace and all related data - brand profile, posts, schedule, media files, and connected‑channel tokens (Facebook/Instagram/Threads/Telegram) - from our database and disk.

Disconnect a single network

To revoke only a connected account without deleting everything: Налаштування → Meta / Threads / Telegram → «Відключити». This deletes the stored access token for that network.

By request (email)

You can also request deletion of your data by emailing o.stepeniev@swipescape.eu from your account email. We process such requests within 30 days.

Facebook / Instagram data

socialio only stores the access token and basic account info you authorize during connection, plus content you create. We never sell or share your data. Removing the app from your Facebook settings → Business Integrations also revokes our access; combined with account deletion above, all associated data is removed.
